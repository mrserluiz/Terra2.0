# Terra Community Packs

[User manual](README.md) · **Terra2 7.0.14-BETA**

Download a compatible original Terra pack and extract it to plugins/Terra2/packs/<pack>/ with pack.yml at the pack root. Use the loaded pack ID, which may differ from the folder name. Original Terra packs do not require conversion.


```text
plugins/Terra2/packs/OVERWORLD/pack.yml
plugins/Terra2/packs/TARTARUS/pack.yml
```
