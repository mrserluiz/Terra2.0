# Vanilla datapack converter

[User manual](README.md) · **Terra2 7.0.14-BETA**

Place a vanilla datapack ZIP or folder with pack.mcmeta in plugins/Terra2/conversion/input/. Convert and inspect its report. READY is executable within the supported subset; BLOCKED is not. Version 7.0.14 is not a universal converter.


```text
plugins/Terra2/conversion/input/
plugins/Terra2/conversion/reports/
plugins/Terra2/terrapacks/

/terra2 convert Plano flat-demo terra2_demo:flat
/terra2 convert Debris scatter-demo
/terra2 packs inspect Plano
/terra2 packs inspect Debris
```
